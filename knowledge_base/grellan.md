# Grellan

Grellans are a species of tall, powerful beings native to the forest world of Vergrove in the Expanse. They are recognized by their imposing height, muscular build, and thick coats of fur, which can range in shade across individuals. Despite their fierce appearance and great physical strength, Grellans are generally known for loyalty, intelligence, and strong social bonds within their communities.

On their homeworld, Grellans traditionally live among the immense trees of dense forests, constructing elevated settlements high above the ground. Their culture places significant value on honor, family, and devotion to companions, and a life-debt owed to another being is treated with deep seriousness. Grellans communicate through a distinctive language of growls and roars that other species often find difficult to reproduce.

Throughout Astral history, Grellans have faced hardship, including periods of oppression under the Helion Dominion, which exploited their strength for labor. Many Grellans resisted such treatment and contributed to broader struggles against Dominion rule. The species is perhaps best represented by Grawl, a Grellan closely associated with Dax Ferro and the Ember Coalition, whose exploits brought the species wider recognition across the Expanse.
