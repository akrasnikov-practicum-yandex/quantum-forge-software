# Fold-drive

A fold-drive is a propulsion system that allows starships to travel faster than light by entering fold-space, an alternate dimension that connects distant points across the Expanse. Without this technology, interstellar travel would take generations; with it, journeys between star systems can be completed in hours or days. The fold-drive is therefore considered one of the foundational technologies of astral civilization.

To make a jump to fold-speed, a ship must calculate a precise route through fold-space, accounting for stars, gravity wells, and other obstacles that exist as hazards in the alternate dimension. A miscalculation can be catastrophic, so navigation computers and astro-core mechitons are commonly used to plot safe courses. Vessels such as the Wraithwind were famous for their unusually fast fold-drives.

Fold-drives vary widely in performance and reliability. Military vessels of the Aurelian Concord and later the Dominion employed powerful, well-maintained units, while smugglers and members of the Ember Coalition often relied on aging or modified drives prone to failure. Because fold-space travel is essential to commerce, warfare, and exploration, control over fold-lane routes has long been a source of strategic advantage throughout the Expanse.
