# Joran Skyrend

Joran Skyrend is a Veyari and a hero of the Ember Coalition in the Astral Strife Expanse. Raised on the desert world of Dunhollow, Joran was the son of Anbar Skyrend and grew up unaware of his connection to the Synth Flux. After meeting the aged Oben-Var Renoth, he learned of the Veyari Conclave and began his journey to oppose the Helion Dominion and its ruler, Overlord Malkor.

Joran joined the Uprising and became a skilled pilot, flying a Kestrel-X during the assault that destroyed the first Void Core. Alongside Princess Mira, Dax Ferro, and the Grellan Grawl, he fought across worlds including icy Glaciax and the forest moon of Verdara. Seeking deeper training in the Synth Flux, Joran traveled to the swamp world of Mirewood, where the ancient Veyari Master Voda instructed him in the ways of the lumen tide and the use of an arc-glaive.

Joran eventually confronted Xarn Velgor, learning that the Drakkar Sovereign was his father, Anbar. His refusal to embrace the umbral tide helped bring about the downfall of the Drakkar and the Dominion.
