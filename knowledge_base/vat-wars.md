# Vat Wars

The Vat Wars were an expanse-spanning conflict fought between the Aurelian Concord and a secessionist movement that sought to break away from central authority. The war drew its name from the vast army of vat troopers created to fight on behalf of the Concord. These soldiers were grown and trained on the world of Tethys, bred for loyalty and combat efficiency, and deployed across countless battlefields throughout the Expanse.

Members of the Veyari Conclave served as generals and commanders during the war, leading vat-born armies into battle alongside fleets of warships. Figures such as Anbar Skyrend, Oben-Var Renoth, and the Kethan Ashka Vyne became renowned for their leadership in the conflict. The fighting strained the Veyari, drawing them away from their traditional role as peacekeepers and into the position of military leaders.

Unknown to most, the entire war was secretly orchestrated by Drakh Umbra, a Drakkar Sovereign operating from within the Synod. By manipulating both sides, he engineered a prolonged conflict that concentrated power in his own hands. The Vat Wars ultimately ended with the destruction of the Veyari Conclave and the transformation of the Concord into the Helion Dominion.
