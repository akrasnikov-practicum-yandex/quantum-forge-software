# Tibbin

Tibbins are a species of small, fur-covered beings native to the forest moon associated with Verdara in the Outer Reach of the Expanse. Standing roughly the height of a child, they have expressive faces, large eyes, and dense coats of fur. Adapted to life among towering trees, Tibbins are agile, resourceful, and closely attuned to the natural rhythms of their woodland home.

Tibbins live in tribal communities housed in elaborate villages built high in the forest canopy, connected by walkways and platforms. Their society is organized around hunting, gathering, and craftwork, and they rely on simple tools, traps, and weapons fashioned from natural materials. Spiritual beliefs and oral traditions play an important role in their culture, and they maintain strong communal bonds and a cautious attitude toward outsiders.

Despite their modest technology, Tibbins proved capable and determined when their home was threatened. During a major confrontation between the Helion Dominion and the Ember Coalition on their moon, the Tibbins allied with the Embers and used their knowledge of the terrain to aid the Uprising. Their unexpected contribution helped the Coalition achieve victory, earning the species a notable place in Astral history.
