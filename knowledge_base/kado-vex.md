# Kado Vex

Kado Vex is a notorious bounty hunter in the Astral Strife Expanse, known for his distinctive armor and reputation for ruthless efficiency. He is an unaltered vat-born of Jarn Vex, a Korrathi bounty hunter whose genetic template was used to create the vat trooper armies grown on the ocean world of Tethys. Raised by Jarn, Kado inherited his combat skills and adopted the armored traditions associated with Korrath.

Operating outside the law, Kado Vex took contracts from powerful clients, including the Helion Dominion and the crime lord Vorbo the Vossk. He was hired to track the Wraithwind and its crew, leading to the capture of Dax Ferro. Kado played a key role in delivering Dax, frozen in cryon, to Vorbo on the desert world of Dunhollow, where the smuggler was held as a trophy.

Equipped with an array of pulse-casters and specialized gear, Kado Vex earned a fearsome standing among bounty hunters. His pursuit of valuable targets repeatedly brought him into contact with Joran Skyrend, Princess Mira, and the Grellan Grawl.
