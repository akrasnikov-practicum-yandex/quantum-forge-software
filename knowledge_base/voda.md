# Voda

Voda is an ancient and powerful Veyari Master in the Astral Strife Expanse, renowned for his deep wisdom and mastery of the Synth Flux. For centuries he served the Veyari Conclave, training generations of novitiates and Padawans and helping guide the Aurelian Concord. Despite his small stature and advanced age, Voda was among the most skilled arc-glaive duelists and the strongest practitioners of the lumen tide in the Expanse.

During the Vat Wars, Voda led vat troopers in battle and served on the Veyari Council. After Overlord Malkor, the Drakkar Sovereign known as Drakh Umbra, issued Directive Null, the Veyari Conclave was nearly destroyed and the Helion Dominion seized power. Voda survived and went into self-imposed exile on the remote swamp world of Mirewood, where he lived in solitude for many years.

When Joran Skyrend sought training, Voda became his teacher, instructing him in the ways of the Synth Flux and warning him against the temptations of the umbral tide. His guidance proved essential to Joran's growth as a Veyari and to the eventual fall of the Drakkar and the Dominion.
