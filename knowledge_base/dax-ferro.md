# Dax Ferro

Dax Ferro is a smuggler turned hero of the Ember Coalition in the Astral Strife Expanse. Known for his sharp wit and independent nature, Dax operated the fast freighter known as the Wraithwind, a ship famous for its powerful fold-drive and ability to reach fold-speed quickly. His closest companion was the loyal Grellan Grawl, who served as his copilot and partner during countless journeys across the Outer Reach.

Initially motivated by payment, Dax agreed to transport Joran Skyrend and Oben-Var Renoth, a decision that drew him into the conflict against the Helion Dominion. He played a crucial role in the destruction of the Void Core and grew into a committed member of the Uprising. Over time, he formed a close bond with Princess Mira and fought alongside the Coalition on worlds such as Glaciax and the forest moon of Verdara.

Pursued for old debts owed to Vorbo the Vossk on Dunhollow, Dax was once frozen in cryon by the Dominion. He was later rescued by his allies and continued the fight against Dominion forces.
