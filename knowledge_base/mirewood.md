# Mirewood

Mirewood is a remote swamp world located in the Outer Reach of the Expanse, covered by murky wetlands, dense jungle, and tangled vegetation. The planet experiences a humid climate, with persistent mist, stagnant water, and abundant plant and animal life. It has no significant settlements or industry and lies far from established trade routes, making it one of the most isolated and overlooked worlds in the Expanse.

The planet's thick atmosphere and pervasive natural energy give it an unusual quality often linked to the Synth Flux. This characteristic, combined with its seclusion, made Mirewood a suitable place of refuge and contemplation. The world's wild, untamed environment offered concealment from outside observation and a setting removed from the conflicts that dominated more populated regions of space.

Mirewood is most notable as the secluded dwelling place of the Veyari Master Voda during a long period of exile. There, in solitude amid the swamps, Voda lived in seclusion and later instructed Joran Skyrend in the ways of the Veyari and the Synth Flux. Because of this association, the otherwise obscure swamp world holds a meaningful place in the history of the Veyari Conclave.
