# Ashka Vyne

Ashka Vyne is a former Veyari and a prominent figure in the Astral Strife Expanse, known for her independence and strong connection to the Synth Flux. As a young Kethan, she was trained by Anbar Skyrend during the Vat Wars, fighting alongside vat troopers in battles across the Expanse on behalf of the Aurelian Concord. Wielding dual arc-glaives, Ashka developed into a capable warrior and a respected leader.

After becoming disillusioned with the Veyari Conclave, Ashka chose to leave its ranks before the rise of the Helion Dominion. When Overlord Malkor issued Directive Null and the Dominion seized control, she survived and continued to oppose Dominion tyranny. Drawing on her training in the lumen tide of the Synth Flux, she aided those resisting the Dominion and worked quietly to support the growing Uprising.

Over the years, Ashka became aware that her former master, Anbar, had fallen to the umbral tide and become the Drakkar Sovereign Xarn Velgor. Her journey took her across the Expanse, where she remained a steadfast opponent of the Drakkar and the Dominion.
