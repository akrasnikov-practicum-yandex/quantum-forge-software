# Mira Auros

Princess Mira Auros is a leader of the Ember Coalition and one of the most influential figures opposing the Helion Dominion in the Astral Strife Expanse. Raised as royalty on the peaceful world of Sennfaltar, she served in the Aurelian Synod while secretly aiding the Uprising. Her courage and political skill made her a key organizer of resistance against Dominion rule and Overlord Malkor.

Mira was captured aboard the Void Core, where she withstood interrogation before being rescued by Joran Skyrend, Dax Ferro, and the Grellan Grawl. After the Dominion destroyed Sennfaltar as a demonstration of the Void Core's power, she became even more committed to the Coalition. She fought in major campaigns, including the defense of the Ember base on the ice world of Glaciax and the decisive battle near the forest moon of Verdara.

A natural user of the Synth Flux, Mira was later revealed to share a deep family bond with Joran and the Skyrend legacy. Her leadership and determination were vital to the eventual fall of the Dominion and the restoration of the Concord.
