# The Synth Flux

The Synth Flux is a mysterious energy field that binds the Expanse together, surrounding and penetrating all living things. Those sensitive to it can learn to perceive and manipulate this energy, gaining abilities that appear almost supernatural. The Synth Flux grants its practitioners heightened intuition, telekinesis, influence over weaker minds, and a deep connection to the living universe around them.

Two great traditions arose around the study of the Synth Flux. The Veyari Conclave followed the Lumen Tide, emphasizing peace, discipline, knowledge, and selflessness, drawing on the Synth Flux to protect and serve the Aurelian Concord. In opposition stood the Drakkar, who embraced the Umbral Tide, channeling passion, anger, and fear to gain raw power. The struggle between these opposing philosophies shaped much of astral history.

Training in the Synth Flux traditionally began in childhood, with a Kethan learning under the guidance of a master such as Voda or Oben-Var Renoth. Mastery demanded years of meditation, study, and discipline. Powerful individuals, including Anbar Skyrend and later Joran Skyrend, demonstrated extraordinary natural ability, while figures like Overlord Malkor wielded the Umbral Tide to dominate the Expanse and crush the Veyari.
