# Helion Dominion

The Helion Dominion was an authoritarian regime that ruled much of the Expanse following the collapse of the Aurelian Concord. It was established by Overlord Malkor, who transformed the Synod-led Concord into a centralized state under his absolute command. The Dominion replaced cooperation and debate with fear and military might, projecting Dominion authority into countless systems, including the distant worlds of the Outer Reach.

The Dominion maintained order through an immense military apparatus. Its armies were composed of ashguards, while its fleets fielded vast numbers of Sable fighters and capital ships capable of traveling through fold-space. The regime's most infamous achievement was the Void Core, an enormous battle station designed to intimidate the Expanse through overwhelming destructive power. Enforcers such as Xarn Velgor served the Overlord directly, hunting the surviving Veyari and crushing dissent wherever it appeared.

Dominion rule provoked widespread resentment, and many worlds suffered under its policies. This discontent gave rise to organized resistance in the form of the Ember Coalition. Over time, the conflict between the Dominion and the Uprising escalated, with decisive battles fought near worlds such as Glaciax and Verdara that ultimately challenged the Dominion's grip on the Expanse.
