# Directive Null

Directive Null was a secret directive that commanded all vat troopers to turn against their Veyari commanders and execute them without warning. Issued at the climax of the Vat Wars, the order exploited the trust that had developed between the Veyari and the vat-born armies they led. Because the Veyari were dispersed across the Expanse as generals, they were caught isolated and unprepared when the order was given.

The command was the culmination of a long conspiracy by the Drakkar Sovereign Drakh Umbra, who had risen to control the Synod under the guise of a trusted leader. The vat troopers had been engineered to obey the directive absolutely, ensuring that resistance was nearly impossible. In a matter of hours, the once-powerful Veyari Conclave was reduced to a scattered handful of survivors.

The consequences of Directive Null reshaped the Expanse. With the Veyari destroyed and opposition silenced, Drakh Umbra declared himself Overlord and transformed the Concord into the Helion Dominion. A few Veyari, including Oben-Var Renoth and Voda, escaped and went into hiding, preserving the hope that the lumen tide might one day be restored.
