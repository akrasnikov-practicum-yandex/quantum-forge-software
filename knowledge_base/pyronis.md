# Pyronis

Pyronis is a volcanic world located in the Outer Reach of the Expanse, defined by rivers of molten lava, smoking volcanoes, and blackened rock. Its surface is intensely hot and largely barren, with eruptions and flowing magma shaping a hostile and ever-changing landscape. The extreme conditions make natural habitation difficult, and the planet has long been regarded as one of the most forbidding worlds in known space.

Despite its dangers, Pyronis's mineral wealth and rare resources drew limited industrial and mining activity over the course of its history. Facilities built on the planet had to withstand searing heat and frequent volcanic hazards. The fiery environment, dramatic terrain, and sense of isolation gave Pyronis a grim reputation throughout the Expanse.

Pyronis carries deep significance in Astral history through its association with the Umbral Tide and the Drakkar. The world became closely linked to Anbar Skyrend, whose transformation into Xarn Velgor is bound to events on the planet. A fortress associated with the Drakkar Sovereign was later established there, reinforcing Pyronis's enduring image as a place of danger, power, and the influence of the Umbral Tide of the Synth Flux.
