# Oben-Var Renoth

Oben-Var Renoth is a revered Veyari Master in the Astral Strife Expanse and a central figure in the history of the Veyari Conclave. A skilled diplomat and arc-glaive duelist, he served the Aurelian Concord during the Vat Wars, leading vat troopers in battles across the Expanse. He was the teacher of Anbar Skyrend, training the young Kethan in the ways of the Synth Flux and the lumen tide.

After Anbar fell to the umbral tide and became the Drakkar Sovereign Xarn Velgor, Oben-Var confronted his former apprentice in a duel on the volcanic world of Pyronis. Following the rise of the Helion Dominion and the execution of Directive Null, Oben-Var went into hiding on the desert world of Dunhollow, where he quietly watched over Joran Skyrend for many years.

Living as a hermit, Oben-Var eventually revealed the truth about the Veyari to Joran and gave him his first arc-glaive. He guided Joran toward the Ember Coalition before facing Xarn Velgor once more aboard the Void Core, becoming one with the Synth Flux.
