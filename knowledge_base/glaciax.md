# Glaciax

Glaciax is a remote ice world located in the Outer Reach of the Expanse, characterized by frozen plains, towering glaciers, and persistent blizzards. Surface temperatures fall far below freezing, and the planet endures long, brutal nights. Its inhospitable climate and distance from major trade routes left it largely uninhabited, with only hardy native creatures adapted to survive the extreme cold.

Despite its emptiness, Glaciax's isolation made it strategically useful. The Ember Coalition established a hidden base on the planet during its struggle against the Helion Dominion, taking advantage of the remote location to shelter forces and equipment. Concealing a military installation amid the snow and ice helped the Uprising avoid Dominion detection for a time, though the harsh environment posed constant challenges to personnel and machinery.

The planet became the site of a significant confrontation when Dominion forces located and assaulted the Ember base. The battle forced the Coalition to evacuate under fire across the frozen terrain. Today Glaciax is remembered as one of the bleakest worlds associated with the Uprising, a frozen wilderness where the conflict between the Dominion and the Embers played out on a desolate, snow-covered landscape.
