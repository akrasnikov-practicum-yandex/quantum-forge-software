# Drakkar

The Drakkar were an ancient order of Synth Flux-wielders who embraced the umbral tide and stood as the great rivals of the Veyari. Where the Veyari pursued peace, discipline, and selflessness, the Drakkar sought power, passion, and domination. They drew strength from emotions such as anger and fear, channeling them into formidable abilities and mastery of the arc-glaive. A fully trained practitioner held the title of Drakkar Sovereign, often taking the prefix Drakh before a chosen name.

For much of their later history, the Drakkar maintained a strict structure of only two members at a time: a master and an apprentice. This arrangement was designed to concentrate power and avoid the internal rivalries that had once destroyed their ranks. The apprentice learned the secrets of the umbral tide while waiting, sometimes for decades, to seize the master's place. Overlord Malkor, also known as Drakh Umbra, was a Drakkar Sovereign who concealed his identity for years while manipulating the Expanse. His apprentice, Xarn Velgor, had once been the Veyari Anbar Skyrend.

The Drakkar pursued a long campaign to undermine the Veyari Conclave and the Aurelian Concord. Through patience, deception, and political intrigue, they engineered the rise of the Helion Dominion, achieving a dominance over the Expanse that the Veyari had long sought to prevent.
