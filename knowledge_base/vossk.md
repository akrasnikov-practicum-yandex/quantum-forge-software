# Vossk

Vossk are a species of large, slug-like beings found across the Expanse, particularly in the lawless regions of the Outer Reach. They are characterized by their massive, elongated bodies, broad mouths, and slow, deliberate movements. Lacking legs, Vossk move by dragging their bulk along surfaces, yet their physical presence and resilience make them formidable figures wherever they hold influence.

Vossk are widely associated with crime, commerce, and the accumulation of wealth and power. Many operate as crime lords, controlling smuggling, trade, and various illicit enterprises through networks of agents, enforcers, and hired bounty hunters. They are known for cunning, patience, and a ruthless approach to business, often surrounding themselves with luxury, servants, and loyal followers while extending their reach over distant worlds.

The species is most prominently represented by Vorbo the Vossk, a powerful crime lord who operated from the desert world of Dunhollow. Vorbo commanded a sprawling criminal organization and exerted considerable control over the planet's underworld dealings. Through such figures, the Vossk became closely linked in the wider Expanse with organized crime, exploitation, and the shadowy economy of the Outer Reach.
