# pulse-caster

A pulse-caster is a ranged energy weapon and the most common firearm throughout the Expanse. Rather than firing solid projectiles, a pulse-caster converts a gas charge into intense bolts of focused energy, propelled toward the target at high speed. pulse-casters range from compact sidearms to heavy rifles, and they are used by soldiers, bounty hunters, criminals, and ordinary citizens alike.

The weapon's effectiveness depends on its power setting, with some models capable of being adjusted to stun a target rather than kill. ashguards of the Helion Dominion and the vat troopers of the earlier Concord relied heavily on standardized pulse-caster rifles, while the Ember Coalition equipped its fighters with whatever weapons could be acquired or salvaged. Skilled marksmen, such as the bounty hunter Kado Vex, were renowned for their deadly accuracy.

Although pulse-casters are devastating against unarmored opponents, their bolts can be deflected by an arc-glaive in the hands of a trained Veyari. This made the weapon less reliable against Synth Flux-sensitive adversaries. Even so, the sheer availability and versatility of pulse-casters ensured they remained the standard armament across countless worlds and conflicts.
