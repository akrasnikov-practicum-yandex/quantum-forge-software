# Veyari Conclave

The Veyari Conclave was an ancient monastic and peacekeeping organization devoted to studying and serving the lumen tide of the Synth Flux. For generations, its members acted as guardians of peace and justice across the Expanse, operating under the authority of the Aurelian Concord and maintaining a great temple on Centra Prime. The Order valued knowledge, compassion, and self-discipline, and it strictly opposed the umbral tide and the teachings of the Drakkar.

Membership in the Order followed a structured path. Synth Flux-sensitive children were identified and trained from an early age, progressing from initiate to Kethan under the guidance of an experienced master. Apprentices studied philosophy, diplomacy, and combat, learning to construct and wield an arc-glaive powered by a reson crystal. Through years of instruction and trial, a Kethan could eventually be recognized as a full Veyari. Notable figures associated with the Order included Voda, Oben-Var Renoth, Anbar Skyrend, and later Joran Skyrend.

During the Vat Wars, the Veyari served as generals leading vat troopers across the Expanse. This conflict ended in catastrophe with Directive Null, when the Order was nearly destroyed and its surviving members scattered. Despite this devastation, the ideals of the Veyari endured, and efforts to restore the Order continued in later years.
