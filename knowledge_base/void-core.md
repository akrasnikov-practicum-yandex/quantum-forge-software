# Void Core

The Void Core was a colossal space station and superweapon constructed by the Helion Dominion. Roughly the size of a small moon, it was designed to project Dominion power across the Expanse through fear and overwhelming firepower. Its most notable feature was a planet-destroying superlaser, capable of channeling enormous energy into a single beam powerful enough to obliterate an entire world.

The station served as both a mobile battle platform and a symbol of the Dominion's dominance over the Synod and the star systems of the Outer Reach. It carried a vast complement of ashguards, support craft, and Sable fighters, making it a formidable presence in any engagement. The Overlord intended the weapon to replace political negotiation with the threat of total annihilation.

Despite its immense scale, the Void Core contained a critical structural vulnerability that the Ember Coalition exploited. A precise strike delivered by starfighters, including the Kestrel-X, triggered a chain reaction that destroyed the station. The loss was a devastating blow to the Dominion and a defining victory for the Uprising, demonstrating that even the most powerful technology could be undone by a determined opponent.
