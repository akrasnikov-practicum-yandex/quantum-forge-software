# Centra Prime

Centra Prime is a densely urbanized world whose entire surface is covered by a single, planet-wide city. Towering skyscrapers, layered transit lanes, and deep lower levels extend far below the cleaner upper districts. Natural landscapes have long since vanished beneath construction, and the planet depends on imported resources to sustain its enormous population. For much of recorded history it served as the political and cultural center of the Expanse.

The planet was the seat of the Aurelian Concord and home to the Aurelian Synod, where representatives from countless worlds debated and legislated. It also hosted the headquarters of the Veyari Conclave, where members of the Veyari trained and coordinated their work across the Expanse. As a hub of administration, trade, and diplomacy, Centra Prime attracted beings from nearly every corner of known space.

Following sweeping political upheaval, including the events of the Vat Wars and the transformation of the Concord, Centra Prime became the capital of the Helion Dominion. Dominion authorities maintained their central command there, and the world remained a focal point of power. Its towering districts and crowded lower levels continued to symbolize both the reach and the inequalities of Astral civilization.
