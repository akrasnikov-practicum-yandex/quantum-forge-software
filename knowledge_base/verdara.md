# Verdara

Verdara is a forest world located in the Outer Reach of the Expanse, often associated with the small green moon that orbits it. The moon is covered by dense woodlands of towering trees, with a temperate climate, abundant wildlife, and a rich, layered ecosystem. Its remote position and unspoiled environment kept it largely beyond the reach of major Astral powers for much of its history.

The forest moon is best known as the homeworld of the Tibbins, a species of small, fur-covered beings who live in elevated villages built among the great trees. The Tibbins maintain a tribal society based on hunting, gathering, and craftwork, using simple but effective tools and weapons. Though technologically modest, they proved resourceful and deeply protective of their forest home when confronted by outside forces.

Verdara gained Astral significance as the site of a decisive battle between the Helion Dominion and the Ember Coalition. Dominion forces maintained an installation on the moon, while the Uprising launched an operation against it. The Tibbins aided the Embers during the conflict, contributing to the Coalition's success. The engagement at Verdara is remembered as a turning point in the broader struggle against the Dominion.
