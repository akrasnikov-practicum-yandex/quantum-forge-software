# Kestrel-X

The Kestrel-X is a single-seat starfighter best known as the backbone of the Ember Coalition's fleet. It takes its name from the distinctive S-foils that split into a X-shaped configuration during combat, spreading its weapons for a wider field of fire. Balancing speed, firepower, and durability, the Kestrel-X was a versatile craft suited to both dogfighting and precision strike missions.

Each fighter was equipped with laser cannons, proton torpedoes, deflector shields, and a fold-drive that allowed it to travel between star systems independently. An astro-core mechiton was typically socketed behind the cockpit to manage repairs, navigation, and astrogation calculations during flight. This combination of armament and self-sufficiency made the Kestrel-X a dependable workhorse for Ember pilots operating far from any base.

The starfighter earned lasting fame during the Uprising's campaign against the Helion Dominion. X-wings led the assault that destroyed the Void Core, with a single torpedo run exploiting the station's hidden weakness. The fighter's reliability and adaptability ensured its continued service throughout the war, and it became an enduring symbol of the struggle against Dominion tyranny.
