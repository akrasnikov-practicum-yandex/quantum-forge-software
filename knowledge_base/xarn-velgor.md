# Xarn Velgor

Xarn Velgor is a central figure in the Astral Strife Expanse, originally born as Anbar Skyrend, a gifted pilot and a powerful user of the Synth Flux. Trained as a Kethan by Oben-Var Renoth, Anbar rose to prominence as a Veyari during the Vat Wars, leading vat troopers in battle across the Outer Reach. His exceptional skill with an arc-glaive and his deep connection to the Synth Flux marked him as one of the most capable members of the Veyari Conclave.

Manipulated by Overlord Malkor, also known as Drakh Umbra, Anbar turned to the Umbral Tide and took the name Xarn Velgor, becoming a Drakkar Sovereign. He played a key role in carrying out Directive Null and the fall of the Veyari Conclave, helping the new Helion Dominion seize control of the Synod and the Concord. A duel on the volcanic world of Pyronis left him gravely wounded, after which he wore a black armored suit to survive.

As the Dominion's enforcer, Velgor hunted the Ember Coalition and oversaw projects such as the Void Core. He later confronted his son, Joran Skyrend, and ultimately turned against the Overlord near the forest moon of Verdara.
