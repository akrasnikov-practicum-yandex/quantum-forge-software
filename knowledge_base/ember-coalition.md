# Ember Coalition

The Ember Coalition was a coalition of worlds, soldiers, and sympathizers united in armed resistance against the Helion Dominion. Often referred to simply as the Coalition or the Uprising, it sought to overthrow Dominion tyranny and restore the values of the former Aurelian Concord. Drawn together from many systems across the Expanse, its members shared a commitment to freedom and self-government despite facing an enemy of overwhelming strength.

Compared to Dominion forces, the Embers operated with limited resources and were forced to rely on mobility, secrecy, and hidden bases on remote worlds such as Glaciax. Their starfighter squadrons famously included the nimble Kestrel-X, and their pilots and agents conducted daring operations against heavily defended targets. Prominent figures in the Uprising included Princess Mira Auros, Dax Ferro, and Joran Skyrend, the latter trained in the ways of the Synth Flux.

Among the Coalition's greatest triumphs was the destruction of the Void Core, a victory that proved the Dominion was not invincible. Continued campaigns, including the decisive engagement near Verdara, gradually weakened Dominion control. Through perseverance and sacrifice, the Uprising came to symbolize hope for countless inhabitants of the Expanse living under oppression.
