# Lyressa

Lyressa is a lush, scenic world known for its rolling green plains, clear lakes, flowing rivers, and graceful cities. The planet features an unusual structure, with bodies of water connecting the surface to vast subterranean spaces. Its mild climate and abundant natural beauty have made it one of the more idyllic and culturally refined worlds in the Expanse.

The surface is home to a human population that values art, architecture, and elegant design, reflected in the ornate styling of its settlements. Lyressa maintains its own systems of governance and has historically participated in the affairs of the Aurelian Concord, sending representatives to the Aurelian Synod. The planet's prosperity and relative stability gave it a respected standing among Concord worlds.

Lyressa holds particular historical importance through its connection to influential Astral figures. It was associated with the early life of a prominent political leader and with the rise of Malkor, who emerged from the planet to become a dominant power and eventually Overlord of the Helion Dominion. Through these ties, the peaceful world of Lyressa became entangled in events that reshaped the entire Expanse.
