# Aurelian Concord

The Aurelian Concord was a democratic union of worlds that governed much of the Expanse for many generations before the rise of the Helion Dominion. At its heart stood the Aurelian Synod on Centra Prime, where representatives from countless systems gathered to debate law and policy. Often called simply the Concord, it embodied ideals of cooperation, diplomacy, and shared governance among diverse member worlds stretching from the core to the Outer Reach.

the Concord relied on the Veyari Conclave to serve as guardians of peace and justice. Acting on the authority of the Synod, Veyari resolved disputes, protected vulnerable worlds, and upheld stability across the Expanse. For much of its history the Concord maintained no large standing army, trusting instead in negotiation and the moderating influence of its institutions to preserve order.

the Concord's decline came through internal division and manipulation. The devastating Vat Wars strained its institutions and justified the creation of vast armies of vat troopers. Exploiting fear and crisis, Overlord Malkor concentrated power within himself and ultimately reorganized the Concord into the Helion Dominion. The fall of the Concord marked the end of an era of democratic rule and the beginning of authoritarian dominance over the Expanse.
