# Reson crystal

A reson crystal is a rare, Synth Flux-attuned mineral that serves as the power source at the heart of an arc-glaive. These crystals possess a natural affinity for the energy of the Synth Flux, allowing them to focus and amplify it into a coherent blade. Because of this connection, reson crystals were highly valued by both the Veyari Conclave and the Drakkar, who depended on them to construct their weapons.

Reson crystals are said to be living in a sense, resonating with the individual who wields them. A Veyari traditionally sought out a crystal as part of their training, forming a bond that determined the color of their arc-glaive blade. The Drakkar, by contrast, dominated their crystals through the Umbral Tide, forcing them to bleed and producing the distinctive red blades associated with Drakkar Lords.

Beyond their use in personal weapons, reson crystals possess immense raw energy when gathered in large quantities. The Helion Dominion harvested vast amounts of reson to power the superlaser of the Void Core, scaling the same principle that gives an arc-glaive its edge into a weapon capable of destroying entire worlds. This made control of reson a matter of strategic importance across the Expanse.
