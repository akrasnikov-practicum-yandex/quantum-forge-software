# Sable fighter

The Sable fighter is the standard starfighter of the Helion Dominion, instantly recognizable by its spherical cockpit flanked by two large hexagonal solar panels. Designed for mass production and speed, the craft sacrificed deflector shields and a fold-drive to achieve a lightweight, agile frame. This philosophy reflected the Dominion's reliance on overwhelming numbers rather than the survival of individual pilots.

Each fighter is armed with a pair of laser cannons and powered by twin ion engines that produce the craft's distinctive screaming sound. Because Sable fighters lack independent fold-drives, they operate from larger carriers, void cruisers, or stations such as the Void Core, launching in coordinated swarms to overwhelm their targets. Dominion pilots were trained to fly in tight formations, compensating for the craft's fragility through discipline and coordination.

In combat against the Ember Coalition, Sable fighters frequently clashed with the more robust and self-sufficient Kestrel-X. While individual Sable fighters were vulnerable, their sheer numbers made them a constant threat. Numerous variants were developed over time, including heavier and faster models, but all shared the iconic silhouette that came to embody the military might of the Dominion.
