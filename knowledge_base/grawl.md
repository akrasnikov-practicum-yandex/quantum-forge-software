# Grawl

Grawl, often called Grawl, is a Grellan warrior and a celebrated member of the Ember Coalition in the Astral Strife Expanse. Hailing from the forest world of Vergrove, home of the Grellans, he is known for his great strength, loyalty, and mechanical skill. Grawl served as the copilot and longtime companion of the smuggler Dax Ferro, with whom he shared countless adventures across the Outer Reach.

Together with Dax, Grawl crewed the Wraithwind, maintaining its temperamental fold-drive and helping pilot the ship through fold-space. He joined the struggle against the Helion Dominion, fighting alongside Joran Skyrend and Princess Mira in major campaigns. His combat ability and devotion made him a valued ally of the Uprising in battles on worlds such as Glaciax and the forest moon of Verdara.

Though Grellans communicate in their own roaring language, Grawl was understood by his closest friends. His bravery contributed to many victories for the Coalition, including efforts that led to the destruction of the Void Core and the downfall of Dominion power.
