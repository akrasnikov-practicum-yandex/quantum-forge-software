# Dunhollow

Dunhollow is a desert world located in the Outer Reach of the Expanse, orbiting twin suns whose combined heat scorches its vast oceans of sand and rocky canyons. Surface water is extremely scarce, and settlements rely on moisture farming to extract humidity from the dry air. The harsh climate, frequent sandstorms, and remote position have left the planet sparsely populated and only loosely connected to wider Astral affairs.

The planet is home to several native and migrant groups. Bands of Dune Stalkers, also known as Dune Stalkers, roam the deep deserts and are known for their hostility toward outsiders. Diminutive Skrets travel the dunes in massive treaded transports, scavenging and trading discarded mechitons and machinery. Scattered farming homesteads and frontier outposts host a mix of traders, laborers, and drifters from across the Expanse.

Because it lies far from major trade routes and central authority, Dunhollow has long been a haven for smugglers, bounty hunters, and criminal enterprises. The notorious crime lord Vorbo the Vossk operated from the planet, exerting considerable influence over local commerce. Dunhollow is also remembered as the early home of Anbar Skyrend and, later, Joran Skyrend, linking this obscure desert world to events of Astral significance.
