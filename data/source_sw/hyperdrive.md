# Hyperdrive

A hyperdrive is a propulsion system that allows starships to travel faster than light by entering hyperspace, an alternate dimension that connects distant points across the Galaxy. Without this technology, interstellar travel would take generations; with it, journeys between star systems can be completed in hours or days. The hyperdrive is therefore considered one of the foundational technologies of galactic civilization.

To make a jump to lightspeed, a ship must calculate a precise route through hyperspace, accounting for stars, gravity wells, and other obstacles that exist as hazards in the alternate dimension. A miscalculation can be catastrophic, so navigation computers and astromech droids are commonly used to plot safe courses. Vessels such as the Millennium Falcon were famous for their unusually fast hyperdrives.

Hyperdrives vary widely in performance and reliability. Military vessels of the Galactic Republic and later the Empire employed powerful, well-maintained units, while smugglers and members of the Rebel Alliance often relied on aging or modified drives prone to failure. Because hyperspace travel is essential to commerce, warfare, and exploration, control over hyperlane routes has long been a source of strategic advantage throughout the Galaxy.
