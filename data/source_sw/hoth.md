# Hoth

Hoth is a remote ice world located in the Outer Rim of the Galaxy, characterized by frozen plains, towering glaciers, and persistent blizzards. Surface temperatures fall far below freezing, and the planet endures long, brutal nights. Its inhospitable climate and distance from major trade routes left it largely uninhabited, with only hardy native creatures adapted to survive the extreme cold.

Despite its emptiness, Hoth's isolation made it strategically useful. The Rebel Alliance established a hidden base on the planet during its struggle against the Galactic Empire, taking advantage of the remote location to shelter forces and equipment. Concealing a military installation amid the snow and ice helped the Rebellion avoid Imperial detection for a time, though the harsh environment posed constant challenges to personnel and machinery.

The planet became the site of a significant confrontation when Imperial forces located and assaulted the Rebel base. The battle forced the Alliance to evacuate under fire across the frozen terrain. Today Hoth is remembered as one of the bleakest worlds associated with the Rebellion, a frozen wilderness where the conflict between the Empire and the Rebels played out on a desolate, snow-covered landscape.
