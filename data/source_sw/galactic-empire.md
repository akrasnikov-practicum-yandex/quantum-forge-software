# Galactic Empire

The Galactic Empire was an authoritarian regime that ruled much of the Galaxy following the collapse of the Galactic Republic. It was established by Emperor Palpatine, who transformed the Senate-led Republic into a centralized state under his absolute command. The Empire replaced cooperation and debate with fear and military might, projecting Imperial authority into countless systems, including the distant worlds of the Outer Rim.

The Empire maintained order through an immense military apparatus. Its armies were composed of stormtroopers, while its fleets fielded vast numbers of TIE fighters and capital ships capable of traveling through hyperspace. The regime's most infamous achievement was the Death Star, an enormous battle station designed to intimidate the Galaxy through overwhelming destructive power. Enforcers such as Darth Vader served the Emperor directly, hunting the surviving Jedi and crushing dissent wherever it appeared.

Imperial rule provoked widespread resentment, and many worlds suffered under its policies. This discontent gave rise to organized resistance in the form of the Rebel Alliance. Over time, the conflict between the Empire and the Rebellion escalated, with decisive battles fought near worlds such as Hoth and Endor that ultimately challenged the Empire's grip on the Galaxy.
