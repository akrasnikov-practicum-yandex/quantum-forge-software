# Kyber crystal

A kyber crystal is a rare, Force-attuned mineral that serves as the power source at the heart of a lightsaber. These crystals possess a natural affinity for the energy of the Force, allowing them to focus and amplify it into a coherent blade. Because of this connection, kyber crystals were highly valued by both the Jedi Order and the Sith, who depended on them to construct their weapons.

Kyber crystals are said to be living in a sense, resonating with the individual who wields them. A Jedi traditionally sought out a crystal as part of their training, forming a bond that determined the color of their lightsaber blade. The Sith, by contrast, dominated their crystals through the dark side, forcing them to bleed and producing the distinctive red blades associated with Sith Lords.

Beyond their use in personal weapons, kyber crystals possess immense raw energy when gathered in large quantities. The Galactic Empire harvested vast amounts of kyber to power the superlaser of the Death Star, scaling the same principle that gives a lightsaber its edge into a weapon capable of destroying entire worlds. This made control of kyber a matter of strategic importance across the Galaxy.
