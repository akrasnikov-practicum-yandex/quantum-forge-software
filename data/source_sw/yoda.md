# Yoda

Yoda is an ancient and powerful Jedi Master in the Star Wars Galaxy, renowned for his deep wisdom and mastery of the Force. For centuries he served the Jedi Order, training generations of younglings and Padawans and helping guide the Galactic Republic. Despite his small stature and advanced age, Yoda was among the most skilled lightsaber duelists and the strongest practitioners of the light side in the Galaxy.

During the Clone Wars, Yoda led clone troopers in battle and served on the Jedi Council. After Emperor Palpatine, the Sith Lord known as Darth Sidious, issued Order 66, the Jedi Order was nearly destroyed and the Galactic Empire seized power. Yoda survived and went into self-imposed exile on the remote swamp world of Dagobah, where he lived in solitude for many years.

When Luke Skywalker sought training, Yoda became his teacher, instructing him in the ways of the Force and warning him against the temptations of the dark side. His guidance proved essential to Luke's growth as a Jedi and to the eventual fall of the Sith and the Empire.
