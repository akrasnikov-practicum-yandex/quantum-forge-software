# Lightsaber

A lightsaber is an energy weapon most closely associated with the Jedi Order and the Sith. It consists of a metal hilt that projects a blade of pure plasma, contained and shaped by a magnetic field. The blade can cut through nearly any material, deflect blaster bolts, and is capable of cauterizing the wounds it inflicts. Despite its destructive potential, the weapon requires considerable skill and discipline to wield safely.

At the heart of every lightsaber lies a kyber crystal, which focuses the energy that forms the blade. The color of the blade is determined by the crystal, with the Jedi traditionally favoring blue and green hues, while Sith Lords typically produce red blades through a corrupted process. Constructing a lightsaber is a personal rite of passage, and a Padawan often builds their first weapon as a sign of growing mastery of the Force.

Lightsabers vary in form, from single-bladed designs to double-bladed staffs and curved hilts suited to particular fighting styles. Because the weapon is difficult to master, it became a symbol of the elite warrior traditions of the Galaxy, distinguishing its wielders from the common soldier armed with a blaster.
