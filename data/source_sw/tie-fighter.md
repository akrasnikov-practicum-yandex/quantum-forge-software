# TIE fighter

The TIE fighter is the standard starfighter of the Galactic Empire, instantly recognizable by its spherical cockpit flanked by two large hexagonal solar panels. Designed for mass production and speed, the craft sacrificed deflector shields and a hyperdrive to achieve a lightweight, agile frame. This philosophy reflected the Empire's reliance on overwhelming numbers rather than the survival of individual pilots.

Each fighter is armed with a pair of laser cannons and powered by twin ion engines that produce the craft's distinctive screaming sound. Because TIE fighters lack independent hyperdrives, they operate from larger carriers, star destroyers, or stations such as the Death Star, launching in coordinated swarms to overwhelm their targets. Imperial pilots were trained to fly in tight formations, compensating for the craft's fragility through discipline and coordination.

In combat against the Rebel Alliance, TIE fighters frequently clashed with the more robust and self-sufficient X-wing. While individual TIE fighters were vulnerable, their sheer numbers made them a constant threat. Numerous variants were developed over time, including heavier and faster models, but all shared the iconic silhouette that came to embody the military might of the Empire.
