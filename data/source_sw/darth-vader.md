# Darth Vader

Darth Vader is a central figure in the Star Wars Galaxy, originally born as Anakin Skywalker, a gifted pilot and a powerful user of the Force. Trained as a Padawan by Obi-Wan Kenobi, Anakin rose to prominence as a Jedi during the Clone Wars, leading clone troopers in battle across the Outer Rim. His exceptional skill with a lightsaber and his deep connection to the Force marked him as one of the most capable members of the Jedi Order.

Manipulated by Emperor Palpatine, also known as Darth Sidious, Anakin turned to the dark side and took the name Darth Vader, becoming a Sith Lord. He played a key role in carrying out Order 66 and the fall of the Jedi Order, helping the new Galactic Empire seize control of the Senate and the Republic. A duel on the volcanic world of Mustafar left him gravely wounded, after which he wore a black armored suit to survive.

As the Empire's enforcer, Vader hunted the Rebel Alliance and oversaw projects such as the Death Star. He later confronted his son, Luke Skywalker, and ultimately turned against the Emperor near the forest moon of Endor.
