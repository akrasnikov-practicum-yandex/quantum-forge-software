# Galactic Republic

The Galactic Republic was a democratic union of worlds that governed much of the Galaxy for many generations before the rise of the Galactic Empire. At its heart stood the Galactic Senate on Coruscant, where representatives from countless systems gathered to debate law and policy. Often called simply the Republic, it embodied ideals of cooperation, diplomacy, and shared governance among diverse member worlds stretching from the core to the Outer Rim.

The Republic relied on the Jedi Order to serve as guardians of peace and justice. Acting on the authority of the Senate, Jedi resolved disputes, protected vulnerable worlds, and upheld stability across the Galaxy. For much of its history the Republic maintained no large standing army, trusting instead in negotiation and the moderating influence of its institutions to preserve order.

The Republic's decline came through internal division and manipulation. The devastating Clone Wars strained its institutions and justified the creation of vast armies of clone troopers. Exploiting fear and crisis, Emperor Palpatine concentrated power within himself and ultimately reorganized the Republic into the Galactic Empire. The fall of the Republic marked the end of an era of democratic rule and the beginning of authoritarian dominance over the Galaxy.
