# Blaster

A blaster is a ranged energy weapon and the most common firearm throughout the Galaxy. Rather than firing solid projectiles, a blaster converts a gas charge into intense bolts of focused energy, propelled toward the target at high speed. Blasters range from compact sidearms to heavy rifles, and they are used by soldiers, bounty hunters, criminals, and ordinary citizens alike.

The weapon's effectiveness depends on its power setting, with some models capable of being adjusted to stun a target rather than kill. Stormtroopers of the Galactic Empire and the clone troopers of the earlier Republic relied heavily on standardized blaster rifles, while the Rebel Alliance equipped its fighters with whatever weapons could be acquired or salvaged. Skilled marksmen, such as the bounty hunter Boba Fett, were renowned for their deadly accuracy.

Although blasters are devastating against unarmored opponents, their bolts can be deflected by a lightsaber in the hands of a trained Jedi. This made the weapon less reliable against Force-sensitive adversaries. Even so, the sheer availability and versatility of blasters ensured they remained the standard armament across countless worlds and conflicts.
