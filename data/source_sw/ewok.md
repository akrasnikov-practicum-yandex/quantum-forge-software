# Ewok

Ewoks are a species of small, fur-covered beings native to the forest moon associated with Endor in the Outer Rim of the Galaxy. Standing roughly the height of a child, they have expressive faces, large eyes, and dense coats of fur. Adapted to life among towering trees, Ewoks are agile, resourceful, and closely attuned to the natural rhythms of their woodland home.

Ewoks live in tribal communities housed in elaborate villages built high in the forest canopy, connected by walkways and platforms. Their society is organized around hunting, gathering, and craftwork, and they rely on simple tools, traps, and weapons fashioned from natural materials. Spiritual beliefs and oral traditions play an important role in their culture, and they maintain strong communal bonds and a cautious attitude toward outsiders.

Despite their modest technology, Ewoks proved capable and determined when their home was threatened. During a major confrontation between the Galactic Empire and the Rebel Alliance on their moon, the Ewoks allied with the Rebels and used their knowledge of the terrain to aid the Rebellion. Their unexpected contribution helped the Alliance achieve victory, earning the species a notable place in Galactic history.
