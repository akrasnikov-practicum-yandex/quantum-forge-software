# Jawa

Jawas are a species of small, hooded beings native to the desert world of Tatooine in the Outer Rim of the Galaxy. They are typically seen wrapped in rough robes that conceal most of their bodies, with only glowing eyes visible beneath their hoods. Adapted to harsh, arid conditions, Jawas are well suited to surviving the scorching heat and scarce resources of their home planet.

Jawas are best known as scavengers and traders. They roam the deserts in enormous treaded transport vehicles, collecting discarded machinery, scrap, and especially droids, which they repair and resell. Their dealings are often shrewd, and they have a reputation for striking opportunistic bargains with moisture farmers and other settlers. Jawas tend to travel and operate in groups, maintaining a clannish and secretive social structure.

The species communicates in a rapid, high-pitched language that outsiders find difficult to understand, and they are generally wary of strangers. Though small and unassuming, Jawas play an important role in the local economy of Tatooine, recycling technology that would otherwise be lost to the sands. Their distinctive appearance and scavenging lifestyle have made them a familiar sight across the desert world.
