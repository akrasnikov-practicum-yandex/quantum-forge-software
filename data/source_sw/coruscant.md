# Coruscant

Coruscant is a densely urbanized world whose entire surface is covered by a single, planet-wide city. Towering skyscrapers, layered transit lanes, and deep lower levels extend far below the cleaner upper districts. Natural landscapes have long since vanished beneath construction, and the planet depends on imported resources to sustain its enormous population. For much of recorded history it served as the political and cultural center of the Galaxy.

The planet was the seat of the Galactic Republic and home to the Galactic Senate, where representatives from countless worlds debated and legislated. It also hosted the headquarters of the Jedi Order, where members of the Jedi trained and coordinated their work across the Galaxy. As a hub of administration, trade, and diplomacy, Coruscant attracted beings from nearly every corner of known space.

Following sweeping political upheaval, including the events of the Clone Wars and the transformation of the Republic, Coruscant became the capital of the Galactic Empire. Imperial authorities maintained their central command there, and the world remained a focal point of power. Its towering districts and crowded lower levels continued to symbolize both the reach and the inequalities of Galactic civilization.
