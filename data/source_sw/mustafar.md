# Mustafar

Mustafar is a volcanic world located in the Outer Rim of the Galaxy, defined by rivers of molten lava, smoking volcanoes, and blackened rock. Its surface is intensely hot and largely barren, with eruptions and flowing magma shaping a hostile and ever-changing landscape. The extreme conditions make natural habitation difficult, and the planet has long been regarded as one of the most forbidding worlds in known space.

Despite its dangers, Mustafar's mineral wealth and rare resources drew limited industrial and mining activity over the course of its history. Facilities built on the planet had to withstand searing heat and frequent volcanic hazards. The fiery environment, dramatic terrain, and sense of isolation gave Mustafar a grim reputation throughout the Galaxy.

Mustafar carries deep significance in Galactic history through its association with the dark side and the Sith. The world became closely linked to Anakin Skywalker, whose transformation into Darth Vader is bound to events on the planet. A fortress associated with the Sith Lord was later established there, reinforcing Mustafar's enduring image as a place of danger, power, and the influence of the dark side of the Force.
