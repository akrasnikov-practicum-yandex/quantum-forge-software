# Endor

Endor is a forest world located in the Outer Rim of the Galaxy, often associated with the small green moon that orbits it. The moon is covered by dense woodlands of towering trees, with a temperate climate, abundant wildlife, and a rich, layered ecosystem. Its remote position and unspoiled environment kept it largely beyond the reach of major Galactic powers for much of its history.

The forest moon is best known as the homeworld of the Ewoks, a species of small, fur-covered beings who live in elevated villages built among the great trees. The Ewoks maintain a tribal society based on hunting, gathering, and craftwork, using simple but effective tools and weapons. Though technologically modest, they proved resourceful and deeply protective of their forest home when confronted by outside forces.

Endor gained Galactic significance as the site of a decisive battle between the Galactic Empire and the Rebel Alliance. Imperial forces maintained an installation on the moon, while the Rebellion launched an operation against it. The Ewoks aided the Rebels during the conflict, contributing to the Alliance's success. The engagement at Endor is remembered as a turning point in the broader struggle against the Empire.
