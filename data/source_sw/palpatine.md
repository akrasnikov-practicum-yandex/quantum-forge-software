# Palpatine

Emperor Palpatine, also known by his Sith identity Darth Sidious, is the principal antagonist of the Star Wars Galaxy and the founder of the Galactic Empire. A master manipulator, he rose to power within the Galactic Senate of the Republic, gradually accumulating authority while secretly serving as a Sith Lord. By orchestrating conflict throughout the Galaxy, he positioned himself to seize total control.

During the Clone Wars, Palpatine commanded vast armies of clone troopers and engineered the downfall of the Jedi Order. He issued Order 66, which turned the clones against the Jedi, and corrupted Anakin Skywalker, transforming him into the Sith Lord Darth Vader. With the Jedi nearly eliminated, Palpatine dissolved the Republic and declared himself Emperor, establishing Imperial rule across countless worlds.

As Emperor, Palpatine wielded dark side powers and oversaw the construction of the Death Star to crush the Rebel Alliance. He sought to turn Luke Skywalker to the dark side, but was ultimately betrayed by Darth Vader during the climactic confrontation near the forest moon of Endor.
