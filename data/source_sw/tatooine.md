# Tatooine

Tatooine is a desert world located in the Outer Rim of the Galaxy, orbiting twin suns whose combined heat scorches its vast oceans of sand and rocky canyons. Surface water is extremely scarce, and settlements rely on moisture farming to extract humidity from the dry air. The harsh climate, frequent sandstorms, and remote position have left the planet sparsely populated and only loosely connected to wider Galactic affairs.

The planet is home to several native and migrant groups. Bands of Tusken Raiders, also known as Sand People, roam the deep deserts and are known for their hostility toward outsiders. Diminutive Jawas travel the dunes in massive treaded transports, scavenging and trading discarded droids and machinery. Scattered farming homesteads and frontier outposts host a mix of traders, laborers, and drifters from across the Galaxy.

Because it lies far from major trade routes and central authority, Tatooine has long been a haven for smugglers, bounty hunters, and criminal enterprises. The notorious crime lord Jabba the Hutt operated from the planet, exerting considerable influence over local commerce. Tatooine is also remembered as the early home of Anakin Skywalker and, later, Luke Skywalker, linking this obscure desert world to events of Galactic significance.
