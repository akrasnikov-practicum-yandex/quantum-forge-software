# Dagobah

Dagobah is a remote swamp world located in the Outer Rim of the Galaxy, covered by murky wetlands, dense jungle, and tangled vegetation. The planet experiences a humid climate, with persistent mist, stagnant water, and abundant plant and animal life. It has no significant settlements or industry and lies far from established trade routes, making it one of the most isolated and overlooked worlds in the Galaxy.

The planet's thick atmosphere and pervasive natural energy give it an unusual quality often linked to the Force. This characteristic, combined with its seclusion, made Dagobah a suitable place of refuge and contemplation. The world's wild, untamed environment offered concealment from outside observation and a setting removed from the conflicts that dominated more populated regions of space.

Dagobah is most notable as the secluded dwelling place of the Jedi Master Yoda during a long period of exile. There, in solitude amid the swamps, Yoda lived in seclusion and later instructed Luke Skywalker in the ways of the Jedi and the Force. Because of this association, the otherwise obscure swamp world holds a meaningful place in the history of the Jedi Order.
