# Ahsoka Tano

Ahsoka Tano is a former Jedi and a prominent figure in the Star Wars Galaxy, known for her independence and strong connection to the Force. As a young Padawan, she was trained by Anakin Skywalker during the Clone Wars, fighting alongside clone troopers in battles across the Galaxy on behalf of the Galactic Republic. Wielding dual lightsabers, Ahsoka developed into a capable warrior and a respected leader.

After becoming disillusioned with the Jedi Order, Ahsoka chose to leave its ranks before the rise of the Galactic Empire. When Emperor Palpatine issued Order 66 and the Empire seized control, she survived and continued to oppose Imperial tyranny. Drawing on her training in the light side of the Force, she aided those resisting the Empire and worked quietly to support the growing Rebellion.

Over the years, Ahsoka became aware that her former master, Anakin, had fallen to the dark side and become the Sith Lord Darth Vader. Her journey took her across the Galaxy, where she remained a steadfast opponent of the Sith and the Empire.
