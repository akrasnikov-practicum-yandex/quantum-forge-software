# The Force

The Force is a mysterious energy field that binds the Galaxy together, surrounding and penetrating all living things. Those sensitive to it can learn to perceive and manipulate this energy, gaining abilities that appear almost supernatural. The Force grants its practitioners heightened intuition, telekinesis, influence over weaker minds, and a deep connection to the living universe around them.

Two great traditions arose around the study of the Force. The Jedi Order followed the light side, emphasizing peace, discipline, knowledge, and selflessness, drawing on the Force to protect and serve the Galactic Republic. In opposition stood the Sith, who embraced the dark side, channeling passion, anger, and fear to gain raw power. The struggle between these opposing philosophies shaped much of galactic history.

Training in the Force traditionally began in childhood, with a Padawan learning under the guidance of a master such as Yoda or Obi-Wan Kenobi. Mastery demanded years of meditation, study, and discipline. Powerful individuals, including Anakin Skywalker and later Luke Skywalker, demonstrated extraordinary natural ability, while figures like Emperor Palpatine wielded the dark side to dominate the Galaxy and crush the Jedi.
