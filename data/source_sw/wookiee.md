# Wookiee

Wookiees are a species of tall, powerful beings native to the forest world of Kashyyyk in the Galaxy. They are recognized by their imposing height, muscular build, and thick coats of fur, which can range in shade across individuals. Despite their fierce appearance and great physical strength, Wookiees are generally known for loyalty, intelligence, and strong social bonds within their communities.

On their homeworld, Wookiees traditionally live among the immense trees of dense forests, constructing elevated settlements high above the ground. Their culture places significant value on honor, family, and devotion to companions, and a life-debt owed to another being is treated with deep seriousness. Wookiees communicate through a distinctive language of growls and roars that other species often find difficult to reproduce.

Throughout Galactic history, Wookiees have faced hardship, including periods of oppression under the Galactic Empire, which exploited their strength for labor. Many Wookiees resisted such treatment and contributed to broader struggles against Imperial rule. The species is perhaps best represented by Chewbacca, a Wookiee closely associated with Han Solo and the Rebel Alliance, whose exploits brought the species wider recognition across the Galaxy.
