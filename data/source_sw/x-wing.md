# X-wing

The X-wing is a single-seat starfighter best known as the backbone of the Rebel Alliance's fleet. It takes its name from the distinctive S-foils that split into an X-shaped configuration during combat, spreading its weapons for a wider field of fire. Balancing speed, firepower, and durability, the X-wing was a versatile craft suited to both dogfighting and precision strike missions.

Each fighter was equipped with laser cannons, proton torpedoes, deflector shields, and a hyperdrive that allowed it to travel between star systems independently. An astromech droid was typically socketed behind the cockpit to manage repairs, navigation, and astrogation calculations during flight. This combination of armament and self-sufficiency made the X-wing a dependable workhorse for Rebel pilots operating far from any base.

The starfighter earned lasting fame during the Rebellion's campaign against the Galactic Empire. X-wings led the assault that destroyed the Death Star, with a single torpedo run exploiting the station's hidden weakness. The fighter's reliability and adaptability ensured its continued service throughout the war, and it became an enduring symbol of the struggle against Imperial tyranny.
