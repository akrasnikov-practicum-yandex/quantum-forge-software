# Sith

The Sith were an ancient order of Force-wielders who embraced the dark side and stood as the great rivals of the Jedi. Where the Jedi pursued peace, discipline, and selflessness, the Sith sought power, passion, and domination. They drew strength from emotions such as anger and fear, channeling them into formidable abilities and mastery of the lightsaber. A fully trained practitioner held the title of Sith Lord, often taking the prefix Darth before a chosen name.

For much of their later history, the Sith maintained a strict structure of only two members at a time: a master and an apprentice. This arrangement was designed to concentrate power and avoid the internal rivalries that had once destroyed their ranks. The apprentice learned the secrets of the dark side while waiting, sometimes for decades, to seize the master's place. Emperor Palpatine, also known as Darth Sidious, was a Sith Lord who concealed his identity for years while manipulating the Galaxy. His apprentice, Darth Vader, had once been the Jedi Anakin Skywalker.

The Sith pursued a long campaign to undermine the Jedi Order and the Galactic Republic. Through patience, deception, and political intrigue, they engineered the rise of the Galactic Empire, achieving a dominance over the Galaxy that the Jedi had long sought to prevent.
