# Rebel Alliance

The Rebel Alliance was a coalition of worlds, soldiers, and sympathizers united in armed resistance against the Galactic Empire. Often referred to simply as the Alliance or the Rebellion, it sought to overthrow Imperial tyranny and restore the values of the former Galactic Republic. Drawn together from many systems across the Galaxy, its members shared a commitment to freedom and self-government despite facing an enemy of overwhelming strength.

Compared to Imperial forces, the Rebels operated with limited resources and were forced to rely on mobility, secrecy, and hidden bases on remote worlds such as Hoth. Their starfighter squadrons famously included the nimble X-wing, and their pilots and agents conducted daring operations against heavily defended targets. Prominent figures in the Rebellion included Princess Leia Organa, Han Solo, and Luke Skywalker, the latter trained in the ways of the Force.

Among the Alliance's greatest triumphs was the destruction of the Death Star, a victory that proved the Empire was not invincible. Continued campaigns, including the decisive engagement near Endor, gradually weakened Imperial control. Through perseverance and sacrifice, the Rebellion came to symbolize hope for countless inhabitants of the Galaxy living under oppression.
