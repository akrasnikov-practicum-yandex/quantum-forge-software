# Hutt

Hutts are a species of large, slug-like beings found across the Galaxy, particularly in the lawless regions of the Outer Rim. They are characterized by their massive, elongated bodies, broad mouths, and slow, deliberate movements. Lacking legs, Hutts move by dragging their bulk along surfaces, yet their physical presence and resilience make them formidable figures wherever they hold influence.

Hutts are widely associated with crime, commerce, and the accumulation of wealth and power. Many operate as crime lords, controlling smuggling, trade, and various illicit enterprises through networks of agents, enforcers, and hired bounty hunters. They are known for cunning, patience, and a ruthless approach to business, often surrounding themselves with luxury, servants, and loyal followers while extending their reach over distant worlds.

The species is most prominently represented by Jabba the Hutt, a powerful crime lord who operated from the desert world of Tatooine. Jabba commanded a sprawling criminal organization and exerted considerable control over the planet's underworld dealings. Through such figures, the Hutts became closely linked in the wider Galaxy with organized crime, exploitation, and the shadowy economy of the Outer Rim.
