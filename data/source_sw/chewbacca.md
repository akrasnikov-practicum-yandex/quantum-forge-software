# Chewbacca

Chewbacca, often called Chewie, is a Wookiee warrior and a celebrated member of the Rebel Alliance in the Star Wars Galaxy. Hailing from the forest world of Kashyyyk, home of the Wookiees, he is known for his great strength, loyalty, and mechanical skill. Chewbacca served as the copilot and longtime companion of the smuggler Han Solo, with whom he shared countless adventures across the Outer Rim.

Together with Han, Chewbacca crewed the Millennium Falcon, maintaining its temperamental hyperdrive and helping pilot the ship through hyperspace. He joined the struggle against the Galactic Empire, fighting alongside Luke Skywalker and Princess Leia in major campaigns. His combat ability and devotion made him a valued ally of the Rebellion in battles on worlds such as Hoth and the forest moon of Endor.

Though Wookiees communicate in their own roaring language, Chewbacca was understood by his closest friends. His bravery contributed to many victories for the Alliance, including efforts that led to the destruction of the Death Star and the downfall of Imperial power.
