# Clone Wars

The Clone Wars were a galaxy-spanning conflict fought between the Galactic Republic and a separatist movement that sought to break away from central authority. The war drew its name from the vast army of clone troopers created to fight on behalf of the Republic. These soldiers were grown and trained on the world of Kamino, bred for loyalty and combat efficiency, and deployed across countless battlefields throughout the Galaxy.

Members of the Jedi Order served as generals and commanders during the war, leading clone armies into battle alongside fleets of warships. Figures such as Anakin Skywalker, Obi-Wan Kenobi, and the Padawan Ahsoka Tano became renowned for their leadership in the conflict. The fighting strained the Jedi, drawing them away from their traditional role as peacekeepers and into the position of military leaders.

Unknown to most, the entire war was secretly orchestrated by Darth Sidious, a Sith Lord operating from within the Senate. By manipulating both sides, he engineered a prolonged conflict that concentrated power in his own hands. The Clone Wars ultimately ended with the destruction of the Jedi Order and the transformation of the Republic into the Galactic Empire.
