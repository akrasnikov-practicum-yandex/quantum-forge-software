# Obi-Wan Kenobi

Obi-Wan Kenobi is a revered Jedi Master in the Star Wars Galaxy and a central figure in the history of the Jedi Order. A skilled diplomat and lightsaber duelist, he served the Galactic Republic during the Clone Wars, leading clone troopers in battles across the Galaxy. He was the teacher of Anakin Skywalker, training the young Padawan in the ways of the Force and the light side.

After Anakin fell to the dark side and became the Sith Lord Darth Vader, Obi-Wan confronted his former apprentice in a duel on the volcanic world of Mustafar. Following the rise of the Galactic Empire and the execution of Order 66, Obi-Wan went into hiding on the desert world of Tatooine, where he quietly watched over Luke Skywalker for many years.

Living as a hermit, Obi-Wan eventually revealed the truth about the Jedi to Luke and gave him his first lightsaber. He guided Luke toward the Rebel Alliance before facing Darth Vader once more aboard the Death Star, becoming one with the Force.
