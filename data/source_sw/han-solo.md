# Han Solo

Han Solo is a smuggler turned hero of the Rebel Alliance in the Star Wars Galaxy. Known for his sharp wit and independent nature, Han operated the fast freighter known as the Millennium Falcon, a ship famous for its powerful hyperdrive and ability to reach lightspeed quickly. His closest companion was the loyal Wookiee Chewbacca, who served as his copilot and partner during countless journeys across the Outer Rim.

Initially motivated by payment, Han agreed to transport Luke Skywalker and Obi-Wan Kenobi, a decision that drew him into the conflict against the Galactic Empire. He played a crucial role in the destruction of the Death Star and grew into a committed member of the Rebellion. Over time, he formed a close bond with Princess Leia and fought alongside the Alliance on worlds such as Hoth and the forest moon of Endor.

Pursued for old debts owed to Jabba the Hutt on Tatooine, Han was once frozen in carbonite by the Empire. He was later rescued by his allies and continued the fight against Imperial forces.
