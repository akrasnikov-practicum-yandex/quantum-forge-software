# Luke Skywalker

Luke Skywalker is a Jedi and a hero of the Rebel Alliance in the Star Wars Galaxy. Raised on the desert world of Tatooine, Luke was the son of Anakin Skywalker and grew up unaware of his connection to the Force. After meeting the aged Obi-Wan Kenobi, he learned of the Jedi Order and began his journey to oppose the Galactic Empire and its ruler, Emperor Palpatine.

Luke joined the Rebellion and became a skilled pilot, flying an X-wing during the assault that destroyed the first Death Star. Alongside Princess Leia, Han Solo, and the Wookiee Chewbacca, he fought across worlds including icy Hoth and the forest moon of Endor. Seeking deeper training in the Force, Luke traveled to the swamp world of Dagobah, where the ancient Jedi Master Yoda instructed him in the ways of the light side and the use of a lightsaber.

Luke eventually confronted Darth Vader, learning that the Sith Lord was his father, Anakin. His refusal to embrace the dark side helped bring about the downfall of the Sith and the Empire.
