# Order 66

Order 66 was a secret directive that commanded all clone troopers to turn against their Jedi commanders and execute them without warning. Issued at the climax of the Clone Wars, the order exploited the trust that had developed between the Jedi and the clone armies they led. Because the Jedi were dispersed across the Galaxy as generals, they were caught isolated and unprepared when the order was given.

The command was the culmination of a long conspiracy by the Sith Lord Darth Sidious, who had risen to control the Senate under the guise of a trusted leader. The clone troopers had been engineered to obey the directive absolutely, ensuring that resistance was nearly impossible. In a matter of hours, the once-powerful Jedi Order was reduced to a scattered handful of survivors.

The consequences of Order 66 reshaped the Galaxy. With the Jedi destroyed and opposition silenced, Darth Sidious declared himself Emperor and transformed the Republic into the Galactic Empire. A few Jedi, including Obi-Wan Kenobi and Yoda, escaped and went into hiding, preserving the hope that the light side might one day be restored.
