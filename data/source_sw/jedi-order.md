# Jedi Order

The Jedi Order was an ancient monastic and peacekeeping organization devoted to studying and serving the light side of the Force. For generations, its members acted as guardians of peace and justice across the Galaxy, operating under the authority of the Galactic Republic and maintaining a great temple on Coruscant. The Order valued knowledge, compassion, and self-discipline, and it strictly opposed the dark side and the teachings of the Sith.

Membership in the Order followed a structured path. Force-sensitive children were identified and trained from an early age, progressing from initiate to Padawan under the guidance of an experienced master. Apprentices studied philosophy, diplomacy, and combat, learning to construct and wield a lightsaber powered by a kyber crystal. Through years of instruction and trial, a Padawan could eventually be recognized as a full Jedi. Notable figures associated with the Order included Yoda, Obi-Wan Kenobi, Anakin Skywalker, and later Luke Skywalker.

During the Clone Wars, the Jedi served as generals leading clone troopers across the Galaxy. This conflict ended in catastrophe with Order 66, when the Order was nearly destroyed and its surviving members scattered. Despite this devastation, the ideals of the Jedi endured, and efforts to restore the Order continued in later years.
