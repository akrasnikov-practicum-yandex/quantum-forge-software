# Boba Fett

Boba Fett is a notorious bounty hunter in the Star Wars Galaxy, known for his distinctive armor and reputation for ruthless efficiency. He is an unaltered clone of Jango Fett, a Mandalorian bounty hunter whose genetic template was used to create the clone trooper armies grown on the ocean world of Kamino. Raised by Jango, Boba inherited his combat skills and adopted the armored traditions associated with Mandalore.

Operating outside the law, Boba Fett took contracts from powerful clients, including the Galactic Empire and the crime lord Jabba the Hutt. He was hired to track the Millennium Falcon and its crew, leading to the capture of Han Solo. Boba played a key role in delivering Han, frozen in carbonite, to Jabba on the desert world of Tatooine, where the smuggler was held as a trophy.

Equipped with an array of blasters and specialized gear, Boba Fett earned a fearsome standing among bounty hunters. His pursuit of valuable targets repeatedly brought him into contact with Luke Skywalker, Princess Leia, and the Wookiee Chewbacca.
