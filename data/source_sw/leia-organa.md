# Leia Organa

Princess Leia Organa is a leader of the Rebel Alliance and one of the most influential figures opposing the Galactic Empire in the Star Wars Galaxy. Raised as royalty on the peaceful world of Alderaan, she served in the Galactic Senate while secretly aiding the Rebellion. Her courage and political skill made her a key organizer of resistance against Imperial rule and Emperor Palpatine.

Leia was captured aboard the Death Star, where she withstood interrogation before being rescued by Luke Skywalker, Han Solo, and the Wookiee Chewbacca. After the Empire destroyed Alderaan as a demonstration of the Death Star's power, she became even more committed to the Alliance. She fought in major campaigns, including the defense of the Rebel base on the ice world of Hoth and the decisive battle near the forest moon of Endor.

A natural user of the Force, Leia was later revealed to share a deep family bond with Luke and the Skywalker legacy. Her leadership and determination were vital to the eventual fall of the Empire and the restoration of the Republic.
